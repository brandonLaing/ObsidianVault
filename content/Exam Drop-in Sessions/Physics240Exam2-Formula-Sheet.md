# Exam 2 Formula Sheet

$g = 9.8$ $m/s^2$

---

## Newton's Second Law

$$
\sum \vec{F} = m\vec{a} \qquad \sum F_x = ma_x \qquad \sum F_y = ma_y
$$

$$
F_g = mg \qquad f_k = \mu_k n \qquad f_s \le \mu_s n
$$

---

## Inclines

$$
F_{\parallel} = mg\sin\theta \qquad F_{\perp} = mg\cos\theta
$$

$$
n = mg\cos\theta \qquad a = g\sin\theta \ \text{(frictionless)}
$$

$$
n = mg\cos\theta \pm F_{\perp} \ \text{(force into / away from the slope)}
$$

---

## Friction on Flat Surfaces

$$
n = mg - F\sin\phi \ \text{(pull above horizontal)} \qquad n = mg + F\sin\phi \ \text{(push below horizontal)}
$$

$$
f_k = \mu_k n \qquad f_{s,max} = \mu_s n \qquad \tan\theta = \mu_k \ \text{(constant speed down a slope)}
$$

---

## Circular Motion

$$
a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} \qquad \sum F_{\text{center}} = \frac{mv^2}{r}
$$

$$
\text{Bottom: } n - mg = \frac{mv^2}{r} \qquad \text{Top: } mg - n = \frac{mv^2}{r} \qquad \text{Top (rope): } T + mg = \frac{mv^2}{r}
$$

$$
v_{min} = \sqrt{gr}
$$

---

## Work and Energy

$$
W = Fd\cos\theta \qquad W_{net} = \Delta KE = KE_f - KE_i
$$

$$
KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \qquad U_g = mgh \qquad U_s = \frac{1}{2}kx^2
$$

$$
KE_i + U_i = KE_f + U_f \qquad KE_i + U_i = KE_f + U_f + f_k d
$$

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2KE}{m}}
$$

---

## Momentum and Collisions

$$
\vec{p} = m\vec{v}
$$

$$
m_1v_{1x} + m_2v_{2x} = (m_1 + m_2)v_{fx} \qquad m_1v_{1y} + m_2v_{2y} = (m_1 + m_2)v_{fy}
$$

$$
v_f = \sqrt{v_{fx}^2 + v_{fy}^2} \qquad \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{v_{fy}}{v_{fx}}\right)
$$

$$
v_x = v\cos\theta \qquad v_y = v\sin\theta
$$
